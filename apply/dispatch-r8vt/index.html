If I join

My first 90 days at Dispatch

A starting plan, which I'd adjust once I've met the team and the customers.

Days 1–30 · Learn

Earn context by shipping

  • Ship a small PR in week one and join the on-call rotation
  • Sit in on customer success calls about NIGOs and integration escalations
  • One-to-ones with every engineer, Product and Design; read every recent post-mortem
  • Map the delivery system as it is today, with no changes yet
Days 31–60 · Tighten

Make delivery predictable

  • Agree sequencing and a weekly cadence with Product
  • Set up incident response and post-mortem ownership the team believes in
  • Headcount plan, job descriptions and interview loop with the VP of Talent
  • Pick two or three metrics customers would feel, such as time to open an account and sync failures
Days 61–90 · Accelerate

Raise the floor

  • First hires closing, with onboarding that gets them shipping in week one
  • AI tooling across the team, measured rather than mandated
  • Remove the process that isn't paying for itself
  • Share a six-month plan for growing toward 20–30 engineers

Hello,

Dispatch is working on a problem I know from the engineering side. Every advisory firm runs CRMs, custodians and planning tools that were never designed to talk to each other, and the data between them has to be right every time. Account opening in under five minutes, 90% fewer NIGOs and advisor transitions four times faster are delivery problems as much as product problems. That's the part of the job I'm best at.

Today I'm Associate Director of Engineering at Spring Health, leading 15 engineers across three teams in the US and Latin America.
